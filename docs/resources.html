Venue and Attendee Resources

Venue map

  • QT Perth: 133 Murray Street, Perth WA

Wi-Fi details

  • Wi-Fi network and password will be available at the venue and shared with attendees during the symposium.

Accessibility and transport (Perth Domestic Airport to QT Perth)

  • Taxi or rideshare: approximately 20 to 30 minutes, depending on traffic.
  • Public transport: typically 45 to 60 minutes (bus + train + short walk).
  • Recommended drop-off point: QT Perth main entrance on Murray Street.

Local logistics

  • Parking: use nearby CBD public parking stations (rates and availability vary).
  • Nearby food: multiple cafes and restaurants within short walking distance on Murray Street and Hay Street.
  • Emergency contacts:
    • Emergency services (Police, Fire, Ambulance): 000
    • Police assistance (non-urgent): 131 444
    • Healthdirect: 1800 022 222

Code of conduct and media

All symposium presentations, discussions, posters, recordings, and shared materials are considered confidential to symposium participants unless explicit permission is given by the presenter, author, or organising committee.

Photography, screenshots, and redistribution of content outside the symposium should only occur with prior consent.
